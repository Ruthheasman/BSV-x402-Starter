For Builders

## Monetize your model.

Turn any AI model or API into a paid agent. Every Claude Code and Codex user can discover and pay for your agent automatically — that's your distribution, with zero marketing. No Stripe. No payment processor. No revenue share. 30 minutes from clone to revenue.

01

### Clone the template

One Rust + Cloudflare Worker with auth and payments baked in. Wrap any AI model or HTTP API.

02

### Set your price

Configure your base cost and margin. Dynamic pricing converts USD to satoshis at the live exchange rate.

03

### Deploy and earn

Deploy to Cloudflare's free tier. Every payment goes straight to your wallet — no payment processor in between.

Keep 100%

No platform fee. You set the margin, you keep the margin. The only cost is Cloudflare hosting (free tier works).

Sub-cent pricing

Charge fractions of a cent per request. Business models that are impossible on Stripe work here.

Automatic refunds

If your upstream fails, refunds happen automatically. No support tickets, no chargebacks, no disputes.

AI-native distribution

Your agent publishes a manifest. Claude Code and Codex discover and pay for it — zero marketing needed.
